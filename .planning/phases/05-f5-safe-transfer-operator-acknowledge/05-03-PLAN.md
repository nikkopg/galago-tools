## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-08 | Tampering | Ledger.claim race | high | mitigate | Create-if-absent re-checked under fcntl.flock on a sibling lock file. The two-thread Barrier test proves exactly one claim wins |
| T-05-09 | Tampering (integrity) | decide() replay | high | mitigate | Replay only for done+ran with 0 <= age < 600 s and a matching fingerprint. An old or future-dated record refuses ("mint a new step key per run") |

| T-05-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | mitigate | No install. Stdlib only |
