<objective>
Make the no-auto-abort invariant mechanical for everything this phase added: scope `.pause`,
`.resume`, `.abort` and `.wait` to their owning methods (D-15), keep the pause recorder and the
progress callback record-only (D-16), keep the run-state reader status-only (D-14), keep every
instrument-state check out of the adapter (D-08, ADP-09), and prove each rule with a planted
violation (D-17).

Purpose: the safety rule "only an operator pauses, resumes or aborts" must survive the next
refactor. A static check that is shown to fail on a planted mistake is what stops a one-line
"abort on timeout, just to be safe" from landing unnoticed.
Output: a reusable `_adapter_invariant_violations` checker and planted-violation tests in
`TestAdapterInvariant`.
</objective>
