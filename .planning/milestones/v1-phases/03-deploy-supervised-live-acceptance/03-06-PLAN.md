  f"run aborted by operator at {result.percent}%" plus ": [outcome]" only when the instrument reported an error text;
  FastServer.Abort sets operator_abort on an accepted abort; the reply code is ERROR_FROM_TOOL.

Design section 6.6: when a step ends Aborted by the operator the queue goes to FAILED with "aborted by operator"; later steps
  do not run; the operator decides retry, skip or clear.

Hazards (repeat to the operator): no Connect / Simulate / "Connect all tools" on the F.A.S.T tool during the run; no
