<objective>
An operator abort ends the step with "run aborted by operator at <p>%", and only an operator abort
does (D-09).

The adapter cannot read from the instrument who aborted a run; it knows only that its own `Abort`
was accepted. So `Abort` records that in the run record, `_run_in_flight` clears it for each new
run, `Transfer`/`RunProtocol` read it once and pass it to a still-pure `_message_for` (D-10). An
abort the adapter did not send keeps today's wording (Core Value: the operator sees what the
instrument did), and TimedOut/Cancelled stay unchanged (D-11). Then the real-core gRPC tests prove
the three cases end to end (D-16) and the invariants are re-confirmed with no new rule (D-17).

Purpose: galago-core shows this text verbatim and moves the queue to FAILED on it (design 6.6).
Output: the operator-abort flag and wording, `TestOperatorAbortWording`, `TestOperatorAbortRealCore`.
</objective>
